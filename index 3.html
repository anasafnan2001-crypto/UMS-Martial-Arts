Led by Grandmaster Um

Grandmaster Um earned his bachelor's degree in Martial Arts at Yong-In University and taught in the Korean special forces before founding UMS Martial Arts in 2001.

Students of all ages and walks of life learn Taekwondo and Hapkido in a close-knit community of instructors and classmates who love what they do.

What students gain

  • Self-disciplineControl in every kick and punch builds respect for rules.
  • Confidence and leadershipDrills teach students to work well with others.
  • Self-controlMind and body training helps release stress and negative energy.
  • Strength and flexibilityStretching, kicking, and conditioning improve health and sleep.
  • Listening and communicationRespect for others is part of every class.
